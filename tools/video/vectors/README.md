# Committed test vectors

Everything else under `tools/video/` is generated by `make-test-streams.sh`.
These three are here because they **cannot be generated**, and a vector that
only exists on one board is a gate that quietly stops existing.

That is not hypothetical. The MPEG-2 results published on 2026-09-17 scored
five streams; by 2026-09-22 two of them (`mm-short.mpg`, `TITLE01-ANGLE1.VOB`)
could no longer be found on the board, on the host, or anywhere in the repo, so
that part of the claim can no longer be re-run. These three were rescued off
the board's `/root/` — not even `/root/video-test/` — before the same happened
to them.

## Why ffmpeg cannot produce these

MPEG-2 field pictures (`picture_structure` = TOP/BOTTOM rather than FRAME).
ffmpeg's `mpeg2video` encoder always emits frame pictures. Tested directly, on
ffmpeg 9.0.1: `-flags +ilme+ildct`, `-vf interlace`, `-field_order tt`,
`-alternate_scan 1` and `-vf tinterlace=4`, alone and combined, every one gives
`picture_structure = FRAME`. Those flags change `progressive_sequence` and the
DCT; none changes the picture structure. `-top` is rejected outright ("not an
encoding option").

This is the coverage that matters most in MPEG-2: **kernel patch 0123**
(`media: cedrus: decode field-coded mpeg2`) exists for precisely this case —
half-height `PICCODEDSIZE` and the held capture buffer. A ladder without a
field-coded vector cannot fail when that patch regresses.

## The files

| file | pictures | structure | role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `m05-720x576-field.m2v` | 62 (31 TOP + 31 BOTTOM) | field, `progressive_sequence=0` | the m05 rung of the decode gate |
| `m05-720x576-field-shortfirst.m2v` | 62 | field | malformed-stream vector, 3,860 bytes removed mid-stream |
| `m05-720x576-field-damaged.m2v` | 62 | field | malformed-stream vector, 395 bytes altered in place |

`m05-720x576-field.m2v` is the file previously called `field.m2v`; the other two
are `field-shortfirst.m2v` and `field-firstfield-damaged.m2v`. Content is
unchanged — verify against the hashes below.

Only the first is wired into `mpeg2-decode-test.sh`. The other two are kept
because they are equally unreproducible: neither is a simple truncation of the
good stream, so whatever produced them understood the picture layout, and that
tool is not in this repo. They are staged for an MPEG-2 malformed-stream suite
(`make-bad-streams.sh` currently covers H.264 and HEVC only).

## Provenance

Original `field.m2v` has no `sequence_end_code`, which is why its last frame
(30 of 31) is not bit-exact — the data is absent, and MPEG-2 defines no
behaviour for truncated input. See `docs/reference/inherited-codecs-2026-09-17.md`
for the analysis that established this is the stream's property and not a
decoder defect.
