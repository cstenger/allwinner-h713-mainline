# Committed test vectors

Everything else under `tools/video/` is generated by `make-test-streams.sh`.
These three are here because they **cannot be generated**, and a vector that
only exists on one board is a gate that quietly stops existing.

These three were taken off the board's `/root/` — not even `/root/video-test/`
— where nothing referenced them and nothing would have regenerated them.

### Correction, 2026-09-23

An earlier version of this file, and the commit message of `44b1ec5`, claimed
that two of the five streams from the 2026-09-17 results (`mm-short.mpg` and
`TITLE01-ANGLE1.VOB`) "could no longer be found on the board, on the host, or
anywhere in the repo". **That was wrong.** Both are in
`local/video-samples/`, next to the `mpeg2_field_encoding.ts` the field-coded
family came from. The search behind the claim covered `local/video-test/` and
the board, and was then generalised to "the host" — a narrow search reported as
a global absence, which is the failure mode this project has a standing note
about. It is corrected here because the commit message cannot be.

The directory was not even hidden. `inherited-codecs-2026-09-17.md` — the
document the whole MPEG-2 readiness review was read out of — names
`local/video-samples/` in the field-pictures section, and has since it was
written. A `grep` for the filenames would have found them in seconds. The
lesson is not "search harder"; it is that a *negative* result deserves at least
the scepticism a positive one gets, especially when it is about to become an
argument.

What was actually true, and is the only part the argument needed: those streams
are **not committed** (`local/` is gitignored), nothing regenerates them, and no
harness refers to them. The gate does not depend on them.

Their provenance, now that it has been looked at properly:
`local/video-samples/md5sum.MPEG2` is the sample-collection manifest from
`samples.ffmpeg.org/MPEG2/` (18 entries — `dvd.mpeg`, `broken-ntsc.mpg`,
`resolutionchange.mpg` and so on).

- `TITLE01-ANGLE1.VOB` — md5 `861f94271c97b03b17773c670a76af7c`, **matches the
  manifest exactly**. An unmodified upstream sample, re-fetchable at any time,
  so it never needed rescuing.
- `mm-short.mpg` — **not** in the manifest, and exactly 2,048,000 bytes. The
  round size and the "short" in the name say it is a local truncation of
  something larger; the source is not identified by anything in that directory.
  Valid MPEG program stream (starts `0x000001BA`).
- `mpeg2_field_encoding.ts` — also absent from the manifest, also a round
  800,000 bytes, same story.

So of the originals, one is recoverable from upstream and two are local cuts of
unidentified sources. That is a smaller problem than "lost", and a real one.

## Why ffmpeg cannot produce these

MPEG-2 field pictures (`picture_structure` = TOP/BOTTOM rather than FRAME).
ffmpeg's `mpeg2video` encoder always emits frame pictures. Tested directly, on
ffmpeg 9.0.1: `-flags +ilme+ildct`, `-vf interlace`, `-field_order tt`,
`-alternate_scan 1` and `-vf tinterlace=4`, alone and combined, every one gives
`picture_structure = FRAME`. Those flags change `progressive_sequence` and the
DCT; none changes the picture structure. `-top` is rejected outright ("not an
encoding option").

This is the coverage that matters most in MPEG-2: **kernel patch 0123**
(`media: cedrus: decode field-coded mpeg2`) exists for precisely this case —
half-height `PICCODEDSIZE` and the held capture buffer. A ladder without a
field-coded vector cannot fail when that patch regresses.

## The files

| file | pictures | structure | role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `m05-720x576-field.m2v` | 62 (31 TOP + 31 BOTTOM) | field, `progressive_sequence=0` | the m05 rung of the decode gate |
| `m05-720x576-field-shortfirst.m2v` | 62 | field | malformed-stream vector, 3,860 bytes removed mid-stream |
| `m05-720x576-field-damaged.m2v` | 62 | field | malformed-stream vector, 395 bytes altered in place |

`m05-720x576-field.m2v` is the file previously called `field.m2v`; the other two
are `field-shortfirst.m2v` and `field-firstfield-damaged.m2v`. Content is
unchanged — verify against the hashes below.

Only the first is wired into `mpeg2-decode-test.sh`. The other two are kept
because they are equally unreproducible: neither is a simple truncation of the
good stream, so whatever produced them understood the picture layout, and that
tool is not in this repo. They are staged for an MPEG-2 malformed-stream suite
(`make-bad-streams.sh` currently covers H.264 and HEVC only).

## Provenance

Original `field.m2v` has no `sequence_end_code`, which is why its last frame
(30 of 31) is not bit-exact — the data is absent, and MPEG-2 defines no
behaviour for truncated input. See `docs/reference/inherited-codecs-2026-09-17.md`
for the analysis that established this is the stream's property and not a
decoder defect.
