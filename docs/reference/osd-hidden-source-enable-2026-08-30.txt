Restore and fault check

Source control restored to 0x03000010; 0x05600014 consumed.

Visual result: vendor logo and red band returned.
Mixer enable remained 0x00001402.
No IOMMU fault or DECD error appeared in dmesg.

Verdict
-------

The video is not merely hidden behind OSD bank 1. Source enable values 1, 2 and
3 are writable and their update latch is serviced, yet none reaches visible
composition with OSD removed. This also repairs a weakness in the 2026-08-26
enable sweep, which changed the shadow register without pulsing 0x05600014.

Commit-latch consumption proves the hardware accepts the source update; it does
not prove that the source is fetched or selected into mixer output. The next
question is the downstream source-service/mixer-selection gate.
